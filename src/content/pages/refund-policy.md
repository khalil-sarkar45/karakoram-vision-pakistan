<h2>Trip-specific terms</h2><p>Cancellation charges depend on services already committed, including permits, hotels, transport, staff and supplier deposits.</p><h2>Written confirmation</h2><p>The applicable payment and cancellation schedule is provided before booking. Travelers should review it before sending funds.</p><h2>Unused services</h2><p>Services voluntarily missed after a trip begins are normally not refundable unless the signed proposal states otherwise.</p><h2>Exceptional events</h2><p>Weather, closures and other events outside reasonable control are handled according to supplier terms and the confirmed service agreement.</p>
