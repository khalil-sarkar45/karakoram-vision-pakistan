  peak, Mashabrum and etc. Majrooh Top is at minimum 4850 meters having less traveled and still
  lesser known trek in the vicinity of Kanday village. Five hours jeep drives take you to the Kanday
  village from Skardu. The trek begins from the village for two days ascend through the summer
  settlements to the top and two days descend to Kanday village for return road journey to Skardu
  and Islamabad.
