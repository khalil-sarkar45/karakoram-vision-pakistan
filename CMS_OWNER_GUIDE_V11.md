# Karakoram Vision Pakistan — Full Flexible Owner CMS V11

Pages CMS edits files directly in the private GitHub repository. A save creates a Git commit; Cloudflare Pages then rebuilds the website.

## Owner control areas

1. **Site Identity, Logos, Contacts & Social** — business name, all logos, favicon, emails, phone, addresses, international messaging apps, social accounts, review profiles, SEO defaults and team members.
2. **Header & Top Bar** — announcement text, hours, contact visibility, support block, search placeholder, language selector and Plan-a-Trip button.
3. **Navbar & Multi-level Menus** — add, edit, delete and reorder main items, second-level items and third-level items. Menu links, stars/icons, visibility and new-tab behavior are editable.
4. **Footer** — newsletter, footer columns, links, contact block, social order, copyright and bottom links.
5. **Homepage Builder** — edit hero copy, enable/disable sections, reorder sections and control card counts, destination cards, trust points, resources, FAQs and final CTA.
6. **Theme** — primary/accent colors, glass header, border, radii, spacing and maximum content width.
7. **Forms** — labels, placeholders, required fields, service options and submit text. Form endpoints remain under Site Settings.
8. **Taxonomies** — owner-maintained lists for difficulty, departure type, service type, region, currency and journey status.
9. **Content collections** — Tours, Trekking, Expeditions, Jeep Safaris, Hunting Services, Travel Guides/News, Vehicles and General/Destination/Hotel/Policy Pages support create, edit, rename and delete.

## Important safety rules

- Use **Draft/Published** instead of deleting content that may already have Google traffic.
- When a URL changes, add a redirect before publishing.
- Do not delete the Site/Header/Footer/Home/Theme/Form settings files; Pages CMS disables deletion for these files.
- Keep image alt text meaningful and upload clean, licensed images.
- Git history provides rollback, but review major menu and URL changes before saving.

## What remains code-controlled

Pages CMS can manage all modeled content and visual settings. A completely new application feature, new database workflow or new component type still requires code. This prevents an owner setting from breaking the full website architecture.
